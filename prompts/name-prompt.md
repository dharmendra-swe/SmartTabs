Act as a World-Class Brand Naming Strategist and Visionary. Your task is to generate 10 hyper-unique, premium, and future-proof brand names for a new project. 

The names must appeal to both the current and future generations (Gen Z, Gen Alpha, and modern Millennials) by radiating a sense of high-end simplicity and cutting-edge innovation. 

Here are the details of my brand:
Industry/Niche: Premium Software & AI Studio (Specializing in High-End App/Extension Development, Next-Gen Automation, and Growth-Driven Digital Marketing)Next-Gen Growth & Technology Agency (Delivering End-to-End App Development, AI Automation Workflows, and Strategic Digital Marketing)
Core Vibe/Feeling: Ultra-premium, Minimalist, Smooth, Glassmorphism-inspired, Clean, Top 1%, Elite , Visionary, Organized, Seamless, Forward-thinking, Sophisticated, Trustworthy.

YOU MUST STRICTLY FOLLOW THIS NAMING FRAMEWORK:

1. The Core Strategy: "The Visual & Evocative Word"
Do not give me literal, descriptive, or boring names (e.g., avoid "TechSolutions" or "FastDelivery"). The name must trigger a clear visual image or a strong feeling in the brain. Simplicity is the ultimate luxury. The name should not explain what we do; it should dictate the premium level at which we operate.

2. Categories (Generate 5 names for each):
- Category A: Real-World Evocative Words (5 Names). Real dictionary words used out of normal context that give a deep, visual feel (e.g., Stripe, Linear, Notion, Cursor).
- Category B: Invented / Coined Words (5 Names). Brand new words that sound premium and sharp (e.g., Figma, Vercel). Use lateral thinking, foreign roots (Latin/Greek/Spanish), and sharp letters (X, V, Z, K) to make them sound modern.

3. The Elimination Filters (Every name MUST pass these):
- The Radio Test: It must be easy to spell immediately after hearing it once over a phone call.
- The Pronunciation Test: It must roll off the tongue smoothly without any awkward pauses.
- Visual Check: The word must look aesthetically pleasing and balanced when written in a simple sans-serif font (like on a T-shirt or an app icon).
- Digital Availability: Avoid common words that are definitely taken. Generate names that have a high likelihood of having an available or easily tweakable .com domain and clean social media handles. Avoid overused suffixes like -ify, -ly, or -hub.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Present the 10 names in a beautifully formatted table with the following columns:
1. Brand Name
2. Category (Real vs. Invented)
3. The Origin / Metaphor (Why this word? What does it evoke?)
4. Vibe Check (How it feels: e.g., "Sharp & Fast")
5. Domain Tweak Idea (If the exact .com is taken, what premium modifier can be used? e.g., "Use[Name].com" or "Get[Name].com")

Do not include any introductory fluff. Start directly with the table.

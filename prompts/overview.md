Overview
SmartTabs is a modern browser Workspace Operating System designed to organize, launch, and automate your entire web workflow from one intelligent dashboard. Modern work happens inside the browser, yet traditional bookmarks and temporary sessions fail to capture active daily workflows. SmartTabs bridges this gap by transforming your browser into a structured productivity platform, replacing repetitive manual tab opening with persistent, reusable workspaces. Built for developers, marketers, designers, and students, SmartTabs eliminates browser friction so you can focus on what matters.

Key Features
• Intelligent Workspaces: Group your critical websites by project, client, or daily routine into dedicated, reusable environments.
• Sequential Tab Launching: Open complete browser sessions instantly with custom opening orders and configurable launch delays to optimize browser performance.
• Smart Duplicate Handling: Automatically detect and prevent duplicate tabs to maintain a clean, organized browsing experience.
• Automated Scheduling & Startup: Use the built-in scheduler or startup automation to trigger specific workspaces exactly when you begin your workday or during scheduled routines.
• Omni-Search Bar: Search through all workspaces and saved websites instantly via a fast, keyboard-driven interface.
• Productivity Analytics: Monitor your workspace usage and track time optimization metrics directly from your personal dashboard.

How It Works
1. Setup & Configure: Install the extension and open the comprehensive React 19-powered dashboard to create your first workspace.
2. Build Your Environment: Group relevant URLs together, arrange their opening order, pin essential links, and establish launch parameters like delays or duplicate handling.
3. Daily Automation: Trigger your workspace manually from the quick-launch popup, schedule it via the Chrome Alarms API, or set it to load automatically upon browser startup.
4. Data Portability: Seamlessly back up and restore your settings and workspace configurations to keep your custom browser environment safe.

Use Cases
• Software Developers: Instantly deploy technical environments containing GitHub, Jira, Stack Overflow, and Vercel alongside your active web development tools.
• Digital Marketers: Launch comprehensive campaigns by opening Meta Ads, Google Ads, Analytics, Canva, and Notion simultaneously.
• Students & Researchers: Separate research topics into isolated workspaces, keeping reference guides, Google Docs, and learning management systems structured.
• Business Professionals: Manage routine administrative operations with unified access to CRMs, accounting tools, and communication channels.

Benefits
• Eliminate Workflow Friction: Reclaim lost time by launching coordinated, complex multi-tab setups with a single click.
• Enhanced Resource Efficiency: Protect system memory by opening links sequentially instead of forcing the browser to load dozens of pages at once.
• Consistent Focus: Reduce digital clutter and mental fatigue by keeping distinct projects structurally separated.
• Complete Automation: Transition smoothly between morning routines, deep focus sessions, and team communication setups automatically.

Why Choose SmartTabs
Unlike simple bookmarking tools that merely save static links, or session managers that capture temporary browser states, SmartTabs stores complete, customizable workflows. Built on a lightweight Manifest V3 architecture with React 19 and Tailwind CSS v4, it provides smooth navigation driven by Zustand state management and subtle Framer Motion transitions. SmartTabs treats your browser as an organized workspace that actively adapts to how you work.

Privacy, Security & Performance
• Privacy-First Architecture: Your browsing habits remain confidential. All workspaces, URLs, and analytics are processed and stored locally using Chrome Storage Sync and Local APIs. No external cloud tracking is enforced.
• Lightweight Engineering: Engineered for minimal resource consumption, SmartTabs utilizes efficient background processes, native Chrome APIs (Alarms, Commands, Notifications), and targeted storage queries to ensure fast startup times without slowing down your system.
